2. Original raw findings before the allowlist (all 5 verified FALSE POSITIVES)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   gitleaks' generic-api-key rule fired on 5 high-entropy strings sitting next to
   key-ish field names. Each was inspected and is a PUBLIC, non-credential value:

   [1] demo/proof.json  "brainKeyHash": "0x...."
       = keccak256(brainKey), a ONE-WAY hash. In demo/run-aura.ts the brain key is
         `ethers.hexlify(ethers.randomBytes(32))` (ephemeral, generated at runtime),
         and only its keccak256 hash is recorded. The key itself is never written to
         any file. A hash cannot be reversed to the key. Public-safe.

   [2..5] images/generation-log.json  "zg-res-key": "<uuid>"  (x4)
       = 0G Compute TEE response-correlation IDs (chatId), the per-request handles
         passed to broker.inference.processResponse() for TEE verification. Not
         credentials; spent per request. The hero's chatId (1ad2b2df-...) is already
         published in demo/proof.json and the README. Public-safe.

   Allowlist (in .gitleaks.toml, line-anchored, scoped to exactly these two shapes):
       "zg-res-key": "<uuid>"
       "brainKeyHash": "0x<64hex>"
   Nothing else is suppressed.
